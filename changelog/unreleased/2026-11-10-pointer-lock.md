- **`El::pointer_lock(f)` captures the pointer for mouse look**: a press on the element hides the cursor, keeps it in the window (locked in place on macOS and Wayland, confined on X11 and Windows) and sends the device's raw motion to `f`, about a point per unit, until Escape, the window losing focus or the element going away gives it back. While captured the pointer stays where the press was, so other buttons and the wheel reach the element too. `Cx::pointer_locked` and `Runtime::pointer_locked` say whether it's held; `Runtime::pointer_motion` and the script event `{"type": "pointer-motion", "dx": …, "dy": …}` feed motion in tests and scripts. Desktop windows only.
