- **`El::pointer_lock(f)` captures the pointer for mouse look**: a press on the element hides the cursor, keeps it in the window (locked in place on macOS and Wayland, confined elsewhere) and sends the device's raw motion to `f` in points, until Escape, the window losing focus or the element going away gives it back. `Cx::pointer_locked` and `Runtime::pointer_locked` say whether it's held, and `Runtime::pointer_motion` feeds motion in tests and scripts. `examples/mouse-look` shows it.
